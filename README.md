# Super Smart Scheduler (S.S.S)

A simple, intelligent scheduler that accepts your tasks, analyzes their urgency, and outputs an organized list and schedule showing you exactly what to prioritize and when to do it.

---

## Overview / Description

Managing daily tasks can be overwhelming. **Super Smart Scheduler (S.S.S)** solves this by taking your input tasks and scheduling them into an optimized list, timeline, and into calender form, helping you stay on top of your deadlines without guesswork.

---

## Features

*   **Add Tasks:** Easily input a task along with its critical details.
*   **List:** View your tasks sorted by custom criteria (e.g., order added, due date).
*   **Schedule:** View a organized version of your tasks arranged by absolute priority and timeline.
*   **Check off task:** Access a dedicated menu to check off completed tasks or review your finished work.
*   **Monthly Calendar:** Visualize your entire month with due dates and scheduled task days marked.
*   **Exit:** Close and exit the application when you are done.

---

## How to Run / Requirements

### Setup Requirements
When you start the program, you will be prompted to enter your **available time for the day (in minutes)**. 

To utilize the **List** and **Schedule** features, you must first add at least one task with the following attributes:
1.  **Task Name** (e.g., *Finish Report*)
2.  **Type of Task** (*Assignment, Project, Exam, or Quiz*)
3.  **Priority Level** (*Scale of 1–5, from lowest to highest*)
4.  **Due Date** (*Remaining days)
5.  **Estimated Time** (*Minutes needed to complete*)

---

## Sample Interaction / Output

When launched, the program will present you with a main menu containing 5 core options: `Add`, `List`, `Schedule`, `Check off task`, and `Exit`. 

Once tasks are added, running the **List** command will render a structured table like the one below:

```
List of tasks: 
+---------------+----------------+-----------+----------------+------------------------+

| Name of task: | Type of task:  | Priority: | Due in (days): | Estimated time (mins): |
+===============+================+===========+================+========================+

| Finish Report | Project        |     4     |        3       |          120           |
+---------------+----------------+-----------+----------------+------------------------+
Total amount of tasks: 1
```
